1. Overview
PDFly is an offline-first PDF and document utility application. Your privacy is central to how PDFly is built: we do not require user accounts, we do not collect personal data, and we do not upload your documents, scans, signatures, or extracted text to external servers.
2. Information We Do Not Collect
PDFly does not collect, transmit, sell, or share:
Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, or account credentials)
Your PDFs, Office documents, images, scanned pages, annotations, or signatures
Passwords you enter to unlock or encrypt PDF files
Precise or approximate device location
Advertising identifiers or behavioral analytics profiles
3. On-Device Document Processing
All core features execute locally on your device, including:
Viewing, searching, bookmarking, and reading PDFs aloud (using Android's system Text-to-Speech engine)
Scanning documents and applying image enhancements
Optical Character Recognition (OCR) using Google ML Kit on-device text recognition
Merging, splitting, organizing, rotating, compressing, watermarking, and converting documents
Drawing, typing, and applying signatures
Password-protecting and unlocking PDFs
When you choose to export or share a document, the file is shared solely with the destination app or folder you explicitly select via the Android system share sheet or file picker.
4. Permissions We Request
PDFly follows a least-privilege approach and only uses permissions necessary for app functionality:
Camera (CAMERA) — Optional: Requested only when you open the Document Scanner to photograph documents. Images captured by the camera remain on your device unless you choose to share or export them.
Internet (INTERNET): Included to allow Google Play Services / ML Kit to fetch on-device text recognition language models if they are not yet cached on your device. PDFly does not transmit your documents or personal data over the internet.
Files & Media: PDFly accesses documents and images only through Android's system document and photo pickers when you select specific files, rather than requesting broad storage access permissions.
5. Local Storage & Data Retention
To support app features conveniently, PDFly stores the following data exclusively on your device in private app storage:
Recent document/tool activity history
Saved signatures you create for signing PDFs
Favorite tool shortcuts and reader bookmarks
App preferences (such as theme mode, default compression level, and default page size)
You control this data at all times:
You can clear history or delete saved signatures from within the app.
Uninstalling PDFly or clearing its app storage in Android Settings permanently removes all locally stored app data.
6. Third-Party Services & Advertising
No Ads or Tracking: PDFly does not display advertisements or integrate third-party ad networks or analytics trackers.
Platform Libraries: On-device OCR uses Google ML Kit, and Read Aloud uses Android's built-in Text-to-Speech service. Any system-level handling by Google Play Services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
7. Children's Privacy
PDFly does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
